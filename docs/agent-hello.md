AI Virtual Office is a monorepo that brings together a modern React+Vite web application, a Hono-based Cloudflare Workers API, and shared TypeScript schemas. The goal is to provide a seamless, AI‑powered virtual office experience where users can interact with intelligent agents, manage tasks, and collaborate in real time. This repository serves as the foundation for building scalable, serverless, and type‑safe applications that leverage AI to enhance productivity and communication.
